# P3.7 — Stream of Spotlights

> **Status:** Historical reconstruction. The original P3.7 file was not recovered; this version preserves the model state documented in January–February 2025.

## Core claim

Consciousness is a **stream of spotlights**. A spotlight may be verbal, visual, spatial, affective, or bodily. The stream is not merely a queue of sensations: it loops backward, compares the present spotlight with recent ones, checks predictions against outcomes, and thereby produces metacognition.

The spotlight metaphor names limited access, not a little person inside the skull holding a torch. Many processes continue outside the focal beam. Conscious contents are the small subset made globally available for comparison, planning, narration, and action.

## Three attentional bands

1. **Primary spotlight** — the content currently dominating report, working memory, and action selection.
2. **Peripheral spotlights** — partially active contents that can rapidly enter the primary beam.
3. **Latent field** — learned models, bodily priors, memories, and sensory processes influencing selection without being directly reportable.

The bands are dynamic. Salience, novelty, threat, reward expectation, bodily need, and deliberate control alter which content wins access.

## Recurrent verification loop

A minimal cycle is:

```text
Sensation / memory / simulation
        ↓
Candidate interpretations
        ↓
Attentional selection
        ↓
Primary spotlight
        ↓
Prediction and action
        ↓
Observed outcome
        ↓
Prediction error and bodily valuation
        └──────────────→ update candidates, priors, and attention
```

Metacognition begins when the system can represent not only an object or event, but also its own prior spotlight, confidence, error, and change of mind.

## Emotion and prediction error

Emotion is not treated as noise added to reason. It is a compressed control signal combining:

- deviation between expected and observed state;
- estimated relevance to survival, attachment, status, and longer-term goals;
- bodily cost and available energy;
- urgency and confidence.

Dopaminergic reward-prediction error is one mechanism in this family, not the whole emotional system. Tonic and phasic neuromodulation alter exploration, persistence, learning rate, and the gain applied to candidate spotlights.

## Executive function as Bayesian competition

Executive control is modeled as a constrained inference-and-allocation process. Candidate interpretations and actions compete under uncertainty. The system approximately asks:

```text
Which model best explains the current signal?
Which action has the best expected value under current constraints?
How much confidence and energy should be allocated before checking again?
```

This is Bayesian in structure, not necessarily because neurons literally solve textbook equations. Priors, likelihood-like evidence, value, and uncertainty jointly determine the winning spotlight and action.

## The self

The self is an **embodied simulation and reference frame** used to bind perception, memory, ownership, agency, and anticipated consequences. It is operationally real without being a separate substance.

A stable self-model helps answer:

- Did I cause this?
- Is this signal internal, external, remembered, or imagined?
- Does this action fit my longer-term goals?
- Is the present spotlight consistent with my recent stream?

Failures of source monitoring, attentional gain, or recursive verification can make internally generated content feel externally imposed or disproportionately real.

## Consciousness as a gradient

P3.7 rejects a crude on/off switch. Functional consciousness varies with at least four dimensions:

1. **Integration** — how many specialized processes can contribute to and use the spotlight.
2. **Recurrence** — whether contents are re-entered, checked, and updated rather than merely passed forward.
3. **Attentional coherence** — whether the stream forms a usable sequence rather than fragmenting into unrelated captures.
4. **Self-reference** — whether the system models its own state, confidence, history, and causal role.

Measures such as information integration, global ignition, recurrent processing, and behavioral self-monitoring can be treated as imperfect probes of these dimensions. None alone proves phenomenal experience.

## Null hypotheses

P3.7 should lose against simpler explanations where they suffice:

- A reportable response may be sophisticated stimulus–response behavior without a rich self-model.
- Recurrence may improve performance without producing experience.
- High information integration may exist in systems nobody would reasonably call conscious.
- Verbal self-description may be imitation rather than introspective access.

## Commitments retained in later models

- physical substrate matters;
- attention is selective and layered;
- conscious processing is recurrent rather than purely feed-forward;
- emotion and homeostasis shape cognition;
- selfhood is a model used for control and attribution;
- consciousness is multidimensional;
- measurable correlates must not be confused with proof of subjective experience;
- panpsychism is not required by the model.
