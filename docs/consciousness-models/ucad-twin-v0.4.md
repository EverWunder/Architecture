# UCAD Twin v0.4

> **Status:** Unified blueprint and operating manual for conscious agency. UCAD combines a structural model with an action-and-governance loop.

UCAD asks a narrower, more operational question than “what is consciousness?”:

> What architecture lets a bounded physical agent sense, allocate attention and energy, manipulate representations, form meaning, act, learn, and veto actions that violate its ethics or long-term telos?

## Structural layers

| Layer | Function | Failure signature |
|---|---|---|
| **L0 — Sensory** | Receives exteroceptive, interoceptive, and system-state signals | Blindness, corrupted input, poor calibration |
| **L1 — Attention** | Selects which signals receive processing and working-memory access | Capture, distractibility, tunneling, missed signal |
| **L2 — Energy budgeting and state modulation** | Allocates metabolic or computational resources; regulates arousal, persistence, and will | Exhaustion, impulsive overdrive, learned helplessness, frozen execution |
| **L3 — Symbols, abstractions, processing units** | Converts selected signals into reusable features and manipulable representations | Category error, brittle encoding, detached abstraction |
| **L4 — Stories, equations, and symbolic manipulation** | Builds causal accounts, plans, simulations, narratives, and formal models | Rumination, confabulation, elegant nonsense, overfitting |
| **L5 — Meaning-making** | Relates the model and action to identity, values, telos, and social reality | Nihilism, ideological capture, goal substitution |

L2–L5 are highly interconnected and recursive. The stack is therefore not a conveyor belt. Meaning alters energy allocation; bodily state changes which story looks true; symbols restructure attention; action changes sensory input.

## DRAC operating cycle

UCAD uses the DRAC loop as its principal control rhythm:

```text
Detect → Regulate → Act → Calibrate
```

### Detect

Acquire the relevant external and internal state. Separate observation from interpretation where possible. Mark missing data and uncertainty.

### Regulate

Set arousal, attention, resource budget, and processing depth to fit the stakes and reversibility of the decision. A high-stakes one-way door deserves more computation than a routine reversible act.

### Act

Commit a bounded intervention in the world. The action should carry an owner, scope, predicted result, reversibility class, and stop condition.

### Calibrate

Compare prediction with outcome. Update the model, confidence, future policy, and—where necessary—the interpretation of the goal itself.

## Ethical-Veto QA rail

Competent agency without a veto rail is merely efficient momentum. Before action, UCAD asks:

1. Is the action within legitimate authority and consent?
2. Is it proportionate to the evidence and risk?
3. Is there a less harmful reversible probe?
4. Does it preserve an exit for affected agents?
5. Would the action remain acceptable if its receipt were public?
6. Is the current state too impaired, captured, or uncertain to authorize it?

A veto does not end the loop. It routes the agent back to detection, regulation, or redesign.

## TABLED alignment matrix

TABLED is used to prevent locally attractive actions from outrunning the wider system. A decision is checked across multiple frames—for example task, actor, body or energy state, environment, long-term effects, and declared telos. The exact labels may vary by implementation; the invariant is multi-frame alignment before irreversible action.

## Instrumentation

### Loop Tightness Coefficient

Measures how quickly and faithfully an action receives external feedback and updates the policy that produced it.

A tight loop has:

- short delay;
- clear attribution;
- low measurement noise;
- a real update path.

A fast loop is not necessarily tight. Rapid repeated action with noisy or ignored feedback is merely a machine gun pointed at uncertainty.

### β·R Spiral Detector

Tracks whether belief intensity or behavioral gain (**β**) is rising faster than reality-testing and corrective evidence (**R**) can constrain it.

The exact implementation is application-specific, but the governing intuition is:

```text
Spiral risk rises when confidence, salience, or action gain increases
while independent reality contact, reversibility, and correction fall.
```

The detector should reduce processing gain, widen evidence sources, and force smaller reversible actions—not pronounce metaphysical verdicts.

### Φ-proxy

UCAD may use a pragmatic integration proxy to estimate how much information is jointly available across layers. It is explicitly a proxy, not Tononi’s exact Φ and not proof of consciousness.

Useful observables include:

- cross-layer mutual constraint;
- recurrence depth;
- persistence across context shifts;
- counterfactual reach;
- self-model use in prediction and correction.

## Twin architecture

“Twin” means the model has two synchronized views:

- **descriptive twin:** the agent’s estimated internal and environmental state;
- **prescriptive twin:** the policy, constraints, and next bounded action.

The two must be reconciled continuously. A descriptive model that never governs action is a museum. A prescriptive model detached from current state is a tyrant with stale maps.

## Relationship to P+

P+ describes how layered cognition and conscious access may be constructed. UCAD asks how those capacities become reliable agency. It compresses some biological detail and adds resource allocation, action, calibration, instrumentation, and an ethical veto.

## Limits

UCAD is an agency architecture, not a proof of subjective experience. A system could implement substantial portions of UCAD functionally while the phenomenal question remains open. Conversely, a conscious organism may have impaired agency, language, abstraction, or action and still deserve moral status.
