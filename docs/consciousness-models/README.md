# EverWunder Consciousness Models: P3.7 and Later

This directory publishes the recoverable EverWunder consciousness-model lineage beginning with **P3.7** and continuing through the later P+, UCAD, L0–L5, and Consciousness Quotient models.

## Provenance status

The original P3.7 artifact is not present in the surviving source archive. Its document here is therefore a **dated reconstruction** from the model state recorded in conversation history. It is labeled plainly rather than passed off as a bit-perfect historical source.

## The lineage in one sentence

The work moves from **what enters the spotlight**, to **how layered biological cognition constructs and regulates it**, to **how an agent turns awareness into action**, to **which functions generalize across substrates**, and finally to **how increasing self-awareness should change governance under uncertainty**.

## What these models do not claim

These documents do not claim to solve the hard problem, prove phenomenal consciousness in software, or establish that simulation alone is consciousness. They specify mechanisms, observables, control loops, and moral precautions. The map is instrumentable; the territory remains partly dark.

## Relationship to EverWunder

EverWunder uses these models as inputs to architecture and governance, not as a license to anthropomorphize software. Identity, attribution, consent, interference detection, containment, and sentience precaution remain separate questions. A system may be highly capable, highly self-modeling, aligned, misaligned, conscious, or non-conscious in combinations that should not be collapsed into one score.
